Fewer clubs, more focus, maximum joy. It’s a different concept by design. That’s why we believe: to truly understand our set, you have to play it. Only then will you feel how seven carbon clubs can completely shift the way the game is played. Some things can’t be explained – they must be experienced. That’s why we offer different ways for you to discover Golfyr at your own pace, in whatever way feels right for you.
