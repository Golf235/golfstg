The Butler

Buy the Set Configure your Set here
Discover more

Always at your service – from fairway to rough.

The Butler is the quiet hero of the #sevenclubgame – always there when you need it. With its wide sole and balanced design, it takes care of the tough terrain: fairway, semi-rough, rough, or tricky lies. Thanks to Carbonics Technology and a fully carbon body, the Butler delivers stability, control, and confidence with every shot. Its shape glides effortlessly through the turf, reduces errors, and supports your natural rhythm, no matter the lie. Just swing, land it solid, no detours.

Why the Butler?

Versatile and reliable
Wide sole for better turf interaction
Stable, balanced body
Versatile and reliable
The Butler is built for all-around-use, bridging the gap between longer and shorter clubs when versatility matters most.
Wide sole for better turf interaction
The specially designed sole helps you move cleanly through the ground, even in tough lies. Less digging, more control.
Stable, balanced body
Its carbon construction allows for optimal weight distribution, giving you greater control and consistency, even on imperfect ground contact.

Where do you play the Butler?

The Butler is your reliable club for mid-range shots and challenging lies.
  • Ideal for long-distance fairway shots and controlled tee shots
  • Designed to glide cleanly through the ground in any terrain
  • Carbon hollow-body construction ensures maximum stability and forgiveness
  • Perfect choice for a confident and versatile mid-range game

Tech Specs

Note: These values are standard specifications and can be adjusted through custom fitting.
Loft
30°
The static loft describes the angle of the clubface when the club is at rest.
Swing Weight
D0
Swing weight describes how “heavy” a club feels during the swing.
Length
38.25″
The length of a club determines how far and how precisely you can hit. Longer means more power; shorter gives you more control.
Lie
61°
Lie describes the angle between the clubhead, parallel to the ground, and the tilt of the shaft towards the hands.
