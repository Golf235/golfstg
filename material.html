Technology

Our Material

Explore Material

A groundbreaking innovation born from expertise. With over 25 years of experience, Golfyr founder Roger Stadler recognized the untapped potential of carbon for golf. Together with carbon expert Daniel Hüsler, he developed Carbonics Technology — a revolutionary manufacturing process that enables the creation of an entire golf club set made from 100% carbon for the first time. The result is lighter, more precise, and performance driven. This innovative material structure ensures optimal weight distribution, maximized energy transfer, and enhanced feel — delivering a completely reimagined golfing experience.

Light and Strong

Carbon is defined by its low weight and exceptional strength. These qualities enable the creation of clubs with strategically distributed weight while ensuring long-lasting durability.

Innovative Design

By utilizing advanced technologies, Golfyr produces club heads with a hollow structure that accounts for only 20-30% of the total head weight. This design allows for precise placement of weight inserts, enhancing stability, accuracy, and optimizing forgiveness.

Energy Transfer

The full-carbon body enables perfect balance and weight distribution within the club, resulting in optimized energy transfer during each swing. This maximizes power while minimizing unwanted vibrations, creating a smooth and satisfying feel at impact.

Authentic Structure

Golfyr uses structural carbon, with its surface texture reflecting the natural outcome of the manufacturing process. This gives the clubs a unique, organic appearance that highlights the material’s authenticity and quality.

Swiss Precision

All Golfyr clubs are entirely developed, designed, and produced in Switzerland. As the only manufacturer of carbon golf clubs in Switzerland and the entire European Union, Golfyr ensures the highest quality standards and precision in production.
