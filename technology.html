Innovation Material Production

The weight
changes everything

Developed and produced in Switzerland, Carbonics Technology is the result of over a decade of engineering — and the foundation of everything we build. By liberating 70% of the clubhead's total weight from the constraints of conventional construction, we place it exactly where it benefits you most. The outcome is a set that is more forgiving and kinder to your body — so you can focus on what actually matters: playing better golf and enjoying every round.

01 / 02

Full Carbon
Hollow-Body

The patented carbon hollow body forms the very foundation of every clubhead. Because carbon behaves elastically where metal is rigid, the entire club absorbs and releases energy far more efficiently than any conventional iron — so off-centre hits still fly straight, slower swings still generate distance, and every strike performs closer to your best one.

  • Maximum energy transfer from club to ball
  • High Smash Factor
  • Absorbs and disperses impact before it reaches your body
  • Significantly reduces strain on your wrists, elbows and back
  • Designed to extend your golfing life
Your advantage on the course
Drastically better results on off-centre hits and slower swing speeds — more distance, more consistency, less effort.
02 / 02

Optimized
Weight Distribution

Because carbon is so lightweight, 70% of the total clubhead weight is freed up to be placed exactly where it benefits you most. For each club, that means individual weight distribution tuned to its specific purpose on the course — maximising stability, reducing twisting on off-centre hits, and delivering a swing that feels balanced from the first stroke to the last.

  • Hitting zone twice as large as with traditional irons
  • Significantly reduced energy loss on off-center hits
  • Consistent high ball speeds even outside the traditional sweet spot
  • Increases MOI by up to 80% compared to traditional irons
  • Reduces twisting on off-center hits
  • Decreases side dispersion by half
Your advantage on the course
A more stable swing and 50% less side dispersion.

With Carbon
golf becomes simpler

A groundbreaking innovation born from expertise. With over 25 years of experience, Golfyr founder Roger Stadler recognized the untapped potential of carbon for golf. Together with carbon expert Daniel Hüsler, he developed Carbonics Technology — a revolutionary manufacturing process that enables the creation of an entire golf club set made from 100% carbon for the first time. The result is lighter, more precise, and performance driven. This innovative material structure ensures optimal weight distribution, maximized energy transfer, and enhanced feel — delivering a completely reimagined golfing experience.

Light and Strong

Carbon is defined by its low weight and exceptional strength. These qualities enable the creation of clubs with strategically distributed weight while ensuring long-lasting durability.

Innovative Design

By utilizing advanced technologies, Golfyr produces club heads with a hollow structure that accounts for only 20-30% of the total head weight. This design allows for precise placement of weight inserts, enhancing stability, accuracy, and optimizing forgiveness.

Energy Transfer

The full-carbon body enables perfect balance and weight distribution within the club, resulting in optimized energy transfer during each swing. This maximizes power while minimizing unwanted vibrations, creating a smooth and satisfying feel at impact.

Authentic Structure

Golfyr uses structural carbon, with its surface texture reflecting the natural outcome of the manufacturing process. This gives the clubs a unique, organic appearance that highlights the material’s authenticity and quality.

Swiss Precision

All Golfyr clubs are entirely developed, designed, and produced in Switzerland. As the only manufacturer of carbon golf clubs in Switzerland and the entire European Union, Golfyr ensures the highest quality standards and precision in production.
