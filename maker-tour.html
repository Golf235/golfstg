The Maker Tour

Developed for Champions.

The evolution of the Maker: Co-Designed with Sergio Garcia. Sergio Garcia doesn't compromise. He came to us with clear demands: what a putter must deliver under pressure, how it should feel in decisive moments. His standards didn't just shape the design — they defined it. The Maker Tour is engineered to those expectations — exceptional stability and high energy transfer through impact, so your putts start on your intended line and travel the right distance, consistently.

Why the Maker Tour?

Co-Designed with Sergio Garcia
Developed in collaboration with professional golfer Sergio Garcia to deliver confidence when the putt matters most.
Exceptional Stability
The hollow mallet design and high MOI of 7'100 maximize forgiveness and keep the face stable at impact.
High Energy Transfer
Tungsten weighting provides clean energy transfer through impact, helping putts consistently travel the correct distance.
Swiss Made & R&A Approved
Engineered with Swiss precision and approved by the R&A for competitive tournament play.

Where do you play the Maker Tour?

You use the Maker Tour on the green whenever precision, touch, and stability under pressure decide the outcome.
  • Perfect for fast, undulating greens requiring precise distance control
  • Provides maximum alignment confidence for crucial short-distance putts
  • Delivers stable roll and forgiveness on off-center hits
  • The ultimate companion to secure your score and finish the hole with confidence

Tech Specs

Note: These values are standard specifications and can be adjusted through custom fitting.
Loft
3°
The static loft describes the angle of the clubface when at rest.
MOI
7’100
The higher the MOI (Moment of Inertia), the more stable the club, resulting in maximum forgiveness.
Head Type
Mallet
Maximizes forgiveness and allows for easy, intuitive alignment.
Head Weight
350g
An increased head weight improves stability during impact.
Hosel
Long Plumber
The hosel is the connection between the shaft and the club head.
Lie
71°
Lie describes the angle between the clubhead, parallel to the ground, and the tilt of the shaft towards the hands.
Who is the
Maker Tour for?
The Maker Tour is made for players who demand professional-level performance and stability on the green. Whether you are battling for a championship or aiming to shave strokes off your game, it delivers the consistent roll and touch needed under pressure.
