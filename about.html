Get to know us

Based in Switzerland, we are redefining the way golf is played. We call it 7>14: an optimized golf experience using only seven essential SWISS MADE carbon clubs on the course.

Our Values

Our approach makes golf simpler, more accessible, and more enjoyable—bringing back the pure joy of the game. It’s time to rethink golf.

Explore our story
2011
An innovator with a vision

The vision of “Rethink Golf” was born, and our founder, Roger Stadler, crafted his first carbon club by hand.

2015
Proof of Idea

We faced the first important question: Is the idea technologically feasible? Roger Stadler reached out to Daniel Hüsler, CEO of adultimum ag and our Head of Technology.

2016
Proof of Concept

The first prototypes were developed under the leadership of Daniel Hüsler – and the vision was starting to take shape.

2017
Club development

In close collaboration between Creative Director Alfredo Häberli and Head of Technology Daniel Hüsler, the club began to take shape.

2019
Proof of Market Fit

We analyzed player habits and market trends, asking golfers: Do you really need 14 clubs, or could you play with fewer? This sparked the idea for the #sevenclubgame.

2021
Proof of Concept #sevenclubgame

After extensive testing and countless challenges, we confirmed that seven clubs were all it takes. The #sevenclubgame was officially born, introducing a revolutionary way to play golf.

2022
Groove Days & Field Tests

In Mallorca, our “Groove Days” confirmed that the engineering theory worked on the course. Afterward, golfers of all handicaps conducted extensive field tests to validate and refine the clubs.

2022
Performance & Durability Testing

We conducted rigorous endurance tests with high-pressure cannons to ensure the durability of our clubs. In parallel, external performance tests confirmed that the Maker outperforms other top-tier putter.

2022
Robot Test in San Diego

At San Diego’s “Golf Laboratories”, our clubs were tested against top competitors, confirming superior accuracy and forgiveness.

2023
Certified for Tournament Play

Our carbon putter, the Maker, has been ruled as conforming to the official Rules of Golf, making it eligible for use in competitive play worldwide.

2023
Market Launch of the Maker

The Maker was ready for launch. We unveiled our carbon putter to the public and press during our Ryder Cup public viewing event in Zürich.

2024
The Maker at LIV Golf

Our 1of7 was seen at LIV Golf in the hands of an international Pro – the Maker is now officially tournament-approved.

2025
Full Set Unveiled: Field Tests Begin

In the scenic setting of Bad Ragaz, our Pre-Order Event brought early adopters together to test the complete Golfyr set on the course, marking a major step from innovation to performance.
